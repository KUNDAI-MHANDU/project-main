As the Lead Developer of Student Aid, I played a central role in designing, building, and deploying a powerful web-based platform tailored to enhance the experience of students living in university residences and navigating new cities.

About Student Aid
Student Aid is an all-in-one platform designed with students in mind. The website primarily offers two key services:

Residence Complaint Management: Students can easily file complaints regarding issues in their university residences. They are able to upload images of broken or damaged equipment, which are instantly viewable by residence managers. This system streamlines communication and ensures faster response times for maintenance and repair work.

City Navigation and Safety: The platform also serves as a city navigation guide for students, providing them with the safest routes between two locations. This is especially useful for students new to the city or unfamiliar with certain areas. By integrating with Google Maps, Student Aid ensures that students can travel smart and stay safe.

My Key Responsibilities
As the sole developer, I was responsible for the end-to-end development of the platform, including both the frontend and backend components. My tasks included:

Frontend Development: Using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I crafted a user-friendly interface that is intuitive and accessible for students of all tech skill levels.

Backend Development: I implemented the server-side logic using PHP and SQL, managing user accounts, complaint submissions, image uploads, and secure data storage.

Map Integration: I integrated the Google Maps API to calculate and display the safest routes across the city, factoring in distance, lighting, and historical safety data.

Mobile Responsiveness: I ensured that the website is fully responsive and performs well on mobile devices, considering that students are more likely to use their phones on the go.

Security and Data Handling: I paid close attention to data validation, user authentication, and secure image uploading to maintain user privacy and platform integrity.

Technologies Used
Languages: JavaScript, PHP, SQL, HTML, CSS

APIs & Tools: Google Maps API, responsive web design tools, image handling libraries

Impact
By streamlining the complaint process and improving student safety through smart navigation, Student Aid has proven to be a valuable tool for student communities. The platform empowers students to take control of their residence experience while promoting safety and awareness in unfamiliar environments.
